# The ladder does not reach — one shortfall under three separate defects

Round 15 delivered the joint lever exactly as ruled and the floor still says nothing about where
people walk. Chasing why produced a root cause that is not a floor finding or a wall finding but
a **palette** finding, and it retires two open questions and one session-two verdict at once.

Instrument: `tools/tier1_floors/measure_ladder_reach.py`. Evidence:
`tools/tier1_floors/evidence/LADDER-REACH.json`, `PATH-SIGNAL.json`, `SEATS-r15.json`.

This supersedes `WALL-SESSION-OPENING.md`, whose anchor figure was a statistic — see §6.

---

## 1. Round 15: the lever fired, and the seat still routed off the walls

Round is **VALID** — the plant seat culled the planted floor and hit the ruin vocabulary.

The joint lever does precisely what the ruling asked, measured on the source before any rig:

| | sheltered joints | trodden joints |
|---|---|---|
| value histogram | **100% at 75.0** | 53% at 101.5, 45% at 113.9 |

Off-route joints deep and dark; trodden joints filled a rung, and nearly half of them packed
level with the stones so the line between two stones stops being a line. That is the ruling,
delivered. Then the blind seat read the lit scene:

> **Q9** — *"The ground told me nothing. I routed entirely off the walls."*
> **Q10** — *"They look the same. The floor has no opinion about its own doors."*

Both are true at once, and the number that reconciles them is the size the ratio never had.

## 2. The joint lever is 1.93× short of the perceptual floor at its **physical ceiling**

| | Weber contrast |
|---|---:|
| what round 15 authored | 0.0270 |
| **the ceiling** — every trodden joint packed level with its stones, every sheltered one at the deepest rung available | **0.0745** |
| §13.8's perceptual floor | **0.1440** |

The ceiling is not a tuning number. It is `joint coverage × the travel a joint owns`: 21.85% of
the surface, moving at most from 75.0 to 113.9. **There is no setting of any joint-confined lever
that reaches the floor in the value channel**, because a fifth of a surface cannot move the whole
surface far enough. Rung one of the pre-declared ladder is exhausted, and exhausted structurally
rather than for want of a harder push.

## 3. …but the joints were never given their travel. The ladder's bottom rung is a clamp

The bond authors a sheltered joint at **0.42 × its stone ≈ 47.8**. The ladder stops at **75.02**.
Every sheltered joint in the world quantises to that one rung.

> **This is gate two's verdict in numbers.** *"All the gaps look standardized… freshly laid and
> mortared, like someone scoured new stone to make it look old."* The differential-wear pass was
> ruled against that and passed in kind — but it only ever varied the **trodden** side. Off-route,
> the histogram is still a single value with zero spread, because the ladder clamps it there. The
> gaps are not the wrong darkness. They are all **exactly** the same darkness.

## 4. The same clamp is what blocks the wall face

The wall session opened by deriving §6.5's stack from the landed floor. Its two lower rows land
under the identical rung:

| plane | ratio | value | nearest rung | |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| floor's sheltered joint | 0.42 × stone | 47.80 | 75.02 | ⚠ **clamped** |
| §6.5 wall top | 1.11 | 117.56 | 114.70 | reaches |
| §6.5 floor | 1.00 | 105.91 | 101.47 | reaches |
| §6.5 wall face | 0.60 | 63.55 | 75.02 | ⚠ **clamped** |
| §6.5 wall face | 0.50 | 52.95 | 75.02 | ⚠ **clamped** |

A wall face cannot be authored on this palette at either end of §6.5's ruled range. The floor's
ladder was derived from floor donors, and a floor has no reason to hold the darkest value in the
room — but its own joints do, and the wall's face does, and neither was there when the ladder was
cut.

## 5. Two rungs, same spacing, same tint, serve all three

| | as shipped (7 rungs) | + 2 rungs below (9) |
|---|---|---|
| sheltered joint 47.80 | clamped to 75.02 | **48.56** ✓ |
| wall face 0.60 → 63.55 | clamped to 75.02 | **61.79** ✓ |
| wall face 0.50 → 52.95 | clamped to 75.02 | **48.56** ✓ |
| joint lever's ceiling | 0.0745 | **0.1253** |

The joint lever's ceiling improves by 68% and lands **within 15% of the perceptual floor** —
close, and still short. Two rungs do not rescue rung one of the fallback ladder; they make the
floor's joints varied again and they make a wall face possible.

## 6. The anchor: the 100.86 that opened the wall session was a statistic, not a moved floor

A median on a quantised surface **is** a rung, and it jumps a whole rung when the 50th percentile
crosses over. Same floor, two field sizes:

| field | median | mean |
|---|---:|---:|
| 12×12 | 113.85 | 107.30 |
| 16×16 | **100.86** | 105.91 |

The median moved 13.0 units — a full rung, 12% — on an unchanged floor. The mean is stable to
1.3%. **The anchor is 105.91**, the area-weighted mean, and it is the right statistic for §6.5 on
its own merits too: the clause separates *planes*, and a median steps straight over the 21.85% of
the floor that is joint. Against the recorded 114.50 the floor has genuinely darkened ~7.5%, all
of it the wear pass deepening the joints — a real move, a third the size the median claimed.

## What this does to the pre-declared fallback ladder

The ladder was *joints → #159 chroma → Ruling 70*. Round 15 discharges rung one on a proof rather
than a shrug, and §2 says no extension rescues it: the value channel is closed to any lever
confined to a fifth of the surface. **Rung two fires.**

Chroma is the right next lever for a reason beyond its place in the queue — it acts on the stone
**faces**, 78.14% of the surface, and the blind seat arrived at it independently without being
asked about colour at all:

> *"Hue runs 27.9°–29.4° and saturation 0.53–0.56 at the 5th–95th percentiles. Every mark on this
> surface is the same colour as the slab under it, differing only in value. That isn't stone —
> that's a single ramp with noise on it."*

A 1.5° hue spread across an entire floor, against §5.4's *chroma is signal*.

## Standing recommendation

1. **Extend the ladder two rungs downward** — same spacing, same tint, nine rungs. It is a
   precondition of the wall family, not an optimisation of the floor, and it separately restores
   variation to every sheltered joint on the floor.
2. **Fire #159 chroma** as the path lever, on the faces.
3. Neither is started. Both want a ruling first, because §5's palette is the one thing every
   other clause is measured against.
