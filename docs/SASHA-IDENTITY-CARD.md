# SASHA — IDENTITY CARD (draft, design thread 2026-09-09)

*Design locks before prompts. This card is settled in conversation, then the Sasha session
fires against it. Rafe's brief, verbatim: "a competent swashbuckler type, a self-assured
hero who looks like he can handle himself, but not in an aggressive way — just in the
understated way that he holds himself, that he moves, the tilt of his head, the well-worn,
non-ostentatious armour and weapons; useful and used, not showy."*

## The one thing his silhouette says
**He can handle himself, and he doesn't need you to know it.** Competence read through
stillness — the same law as the orcs' heraldic stance (§7.4) for the opposite reason:
theirs is stillness-as-menace; his is stillness-as-readiness.

## Body (from the story bible, §1)
- Forty-two, **small**, dark-haired, **dark-skinned** (Revenese, south-coast, sun-cured).
  Not bulky — the opposite of the orcs' Warcraft-skeleton mass. A professional's economy:
  compact, nothing wasted. He reads *smaller* than the things he's walking past, and more
  capable than his size.
- **The knee.** Six years old, never quite forgave a job. At 32px this is not drawn — it is
  *stood*: weight settled slightly off one leg. It's the asymmetry that makes the stance
  his instead of a template's. (Frame budget permitting; see §9 below.)
- **The tilt of the head** — Rafe's words, and the whole expression budget. See Expression.

## Posture and movement
- Idle: weight planted, shoulders down, head slightly tilted — *attention*, not menace. A
  man noticing the wet boot before the danger (story bible: "he lives in texture").
- Walk: unhurried, economical. Not a stride, not a creep. Someone who's walked into worse
  rooms.
- Attack: one motion, no wind-up theatre. The work, not a fight (he never says "combat").
- Hit: absorbs, doesn't flail.

## Gear — useful and used, never showy (§7, §8 applied to a person)
- **Armour:** worn leather/cloth, no plate, no ornament. Wear where wear happens: shoulders,
  forearms, the knee. Nothing decorative, nothing new.
- **Weapon:** [RAFE TO RULE — see questions] — well-worn grip, no jewel, no glow.
- **The wand — Hollowmark.** Brass, at his hip, *always present in every frame.* She is a
  character and she is on screen 100% of the time. She is also the natural home of the
  **warmth channel** (below) and possibly the game's single saturated accent.
- Pack/kit: minimal, professional. He carries what the work needs.

## Warmth channel (§5.4 chroma-is-signal; Sasha + Hollowmark are the warmest thing on
## screen, always — bible §1/§5)
- Warmth lives in **skin** (Revenese, warm-toned) and **brass** (the wand). Everything
  else on him is desaturated toward the spine. Against the Boundary's fires — the only
  other warm thing — he must still read as the *warmest*, not the brightest (#183 lesson:
  lit, not blown). Authored *with* the orc fire, not before it.
- **Hero light response** (#183, re-derived): peak above the lit floor beside him, below
  the shoulder's knee. The light-bearer is lit.

## Expression
- The world has a zero expression budget (§1.1). Sasha is the one exception candidate — and
  at 32px his "expression" *is* the head tilt and the stance. Ruling proposal: **his
  expression budget is spent on posture, never on a drawn face.** No readable mouth or
  eyes doing acting; the tilt does the work. (Face detail is a palette/identifiability
  question at 1×, not an emotion channel.)

## Anti-tells — what he must never look like
- No hero glow, rim light, or aura. No cape flourish. No oversized weapon. No chosen-one
  silhouette. No "adventurer" cheer (Oryx anti-reference, §1.3). No aggression in the
  idle — no ready-stance, no bared weapon at rest.
- The swashbuckler without the swash: if a frame looks like a poster, it's wrong.

## Rig — one-way door (§9, §10)
- **Attachment points, from frame one:** weapon hand, off hand, hip (the wand — fixed),
  back (pack), head (hood/hat if any). Equipment layered by silhouette class.
- **Frame budget (§9.1): idle 2, walk 2, attack 1–2, hit 1 — six to eight. That is the
  set; additions displace.** The idle pair is where the tilt and the weight-shift live.
- Facing: [RAFE TO RULE — 4-direction or fewer; portrait top-down].

## Sequencing
Room (done) → props incl. the orc fire (firing) → palette lock (§5.1) → orc as pipeline
rehearsal (skeleton/rotation/attachment tools proven on a body nobody's attached to) →
**Sasha**.

## Questions for Rafe (rule these and the card locks)
1. **Weapon:** sword (swashbuckler-literal), or knife/short blade (the story's "specialist"
   — a killer's tool, not a duelist's)? This decides the whole silhouette's second line.
2. **Head:** bare, hooded, or a worn hat? Hood reads "hides"; bare reads "doesn't need to."
3. **Expression ruling:** confirm posture-only (no drawn facial acting), or keep a small
   face budget?
4. **Facings:** four directions, or two (mirrored) — the cheaper rig is also the more
   heraldic one.
5. **The knee:** worth a frame's worth of stance asymmetry, or too subtle at 32px to spend on?
