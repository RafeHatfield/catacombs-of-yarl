- [ ] DEBT-020 (#72): Damaged-enemy HP bar not drawn on mid-run resume until the next turn
  - File: `src/Presentation/Main.cs` — `SetupPresentation` (~742) creates a fresh
    `FloatingHpBarManager` (~949) but never calls `Refresh` on it. The bars are only refreshed on the
    per-turn path (`OnTurnCompleted`, calls at ~918 / ~1693).
  - Symptom: after force-killing mid-fight and resuming, an already-damaged monster shows no floating
    HP bar until the player acts once (the next `OnTurnCompleted` redraws it). Data resumed correctly —
    HP is right, only the bar is missing — so this is purely a missing redraw on the resume/floor-entry
    path. Invisible on a fresh floor (all monsters full HP → no bar due).
  - Fix (one line): call `_floatingHpBars?.Refresh(state, _entitySprites);` at the END of
    `SetupPresentation`, after the entity sprites + bar manager exist. Presentation-only; no
    logic/balance. Also benefits any floor-entry that begins with pre-damaged monsters.
  - Verify: rebuild to device, force-kill mid-fight with a wounded enemy, resume → bar shows immediately.

- [ ] DEBT-021 (#73): Explore mode enters with the camera off-screen (regression)
  - Files: entry via `Main.StartDungeon(explorationMode: true)` (~722) → `SetupPresentation` (~742);
    camera transform is `_gameView.Position` (drag-pan at ~1108/1147). The initial center-on-player
    (Camera2D / renderer follow) is not landing on entry in explore mode.
  - Symptom: entering explore mode, the POV starts off-screen — the generated floor isn't visible until
    you pan/scroll a little. Rafe: "an older bug we've reintroduced." Explore mode is a visual
    inspection tool (no monsters), not a campaign run, so player-facing impact is low.
  - Likely origin: bisect the camera-centering path. The recent "production render seam" art work
    (9a2483b / e6a1038) is a more plausible source than the save/resume PRs (#21 / bdc3976), which
    touched neither camera nor explore rendering.
  - Fix direction: ensure the camera recenters on the player (or floor centroid) at the end of
    explore-mode entry, once the tilemap/viewport is laid out (a one-frame-deferred recenter if the
    layout isn't ready synchronously).
