- Nothing in this set is fastened. Add explicit fixings that cross a joint: a driven iron pin whose head sits proud of the block face with a 1px bright rim and a matching darker seat where it entered, and a rope or hide strap that passes over a block, tightens visibly at a peg, and leaves the block behind it deformed by the pressure.
- r09_01, r09_03, r09_04 and r09_08 fill blocks with a single flat colour. Give each block two or three tones of the same hue in irregular patches — the pitted face, the sheared face, the rubbed-smooth band at hand height — not a gradient and not noise.
