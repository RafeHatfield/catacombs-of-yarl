FLIP LIST
- Every tile in this set puts its dark band along the bottom and nothing along the top. Move it: give each tile a 3–4px band along its **top** edge, drawn as the wall's top surface — a distinct value from the front face, with the front face's joints running under it and stopping at it, so the wall has a visible thickness you look at the side of.
- r04_06: break the ruled grid. Vary the fill value of individual blocks by ±10–15 luminance, offset each course horizontally by an irregular amount rather than aligning them, and let joint lines drop or thicken by a pixel where a block has spalled. Then run the vertical post edge-to-edge, top to bottom of the tile, so it continues into the copies above and below.
- r04_06 / r04_04: the vertical member currently ends in mid-air. Take it full height and add a fixing where it crosses each course — a 2x2 driven pin head with one pixel of dark under it, plus a 1px rope lash wrapping the post at one crossing.
- r04_08: kill the single crack. Replace it with three or four short, unaligned fractures no more than 8px long, none touching a tile edge at a memorable point, so no continuous diagonal can form across copies. Also repaint pixel (5,23) to the local wall value — it is currently (178,211,159) and out of palette.
- r04_02: destroy the left-right mirror. Shift the right-hand pale blocks down two rows and in three columns, delete one of them entirely, and replace the centred pale bar with an off-centre timber whose ends are cut by the tile edges.
- r04_07: remove the bordered box. If a fitting belongs there, put it off-centre, let it be clipped by a tile edge, and drop the dark surround ring — let the fitting meet the wall directly with its own joint line rather than a ring of background.
- r04_09 / r04_01: delete the uniform grey field. Fill the entire 32x32 with wall material edge to edge — courses, joints and fixings must run off all four sides — so nothing sits *on* anything.
- r04_05: the flat middle is empty, not restrained. Fill it with the actual repair vocabulary at readable scale — a salvaged timber baulk two-thirds the tile width with a driven pin at each end, and a hide patch stretched over a gap with three lash points — instead of a 1px line drawing.
- r04_00 / r04_03: replace the amorphous mottle with named structure. Decide what the wall is made of (cut block, rubble-and-mortar, or timber over fill), then draw the joints that separate one unit from the next, at least six of them per tile, running off at least two edges.
- r04_03: the flat dark bottom seven rows are unusable as wall. Either cut it to a 2px joint line or fill it with the same block structure as the rest of the tile at a lower value, with the joint pattern continuing through it.
