I read all 40 PNGs at 1:1, magnified the ambiguous regions, and ran pixel diffs across the set. Two facts drive most of this: **cand_13 and cand_33 are byte-identical**, and **cand_03/07/10/18/30 differ from each other by 4–11% of their pixels** — the set has fewer distinct pieces than it has files.

The decisive finding is projection. The brief says these are drawn from above and slightly in front. They are not. On the 12-wide pieces — the ones that must represent wall running away from the camera — there is no receding top strip; cand_15 magnified is horizontal courses and a vertical post, a front elevation exactly like the 32-wide pieces, just narrower. The 4px pale band at the top of every piece appears identically on both orientations, so it is a trim beam on the face, not a foreshortened top plane. Nothing in this kit has a top you could set anything on.

FLIP LIST
- Every piece is a front elevation. Redraw with an actual top plane: for a wall running left-right, 5–7 native pixels of top surface at the top of the tile in a value clearly separated from the front face, with the front face starting below it. Test it by asking whether a lantern sprite could sit on that band.
- The 12-wide pieces must show a wall running away from the camera, not a narrow front elevation. Redraw them as a top strip running the full height of the tile with a thin front face only at the near end — not as horizontal courses top to bottom (cand_15, cand_02, cand_13, cand_31).
- On the corner pieces, draw the returning face: where the run turns, show 5–7 pixels of the second wall's top plane meeting the first at a visible mitre or a butt joint. Right now the turn exists only in the silhouette (cand_01, cand_04, cand_14, cand_17, cand_22, cand_27, cand_36, cand_39).
- On the doorway pieces, put a reveal in the opening: 3–4 pixels of wall depth on each jamb face and a soffit under the lintel, in a darker value than the front face. Currently the opening's cut faces are blank alpha (cand_21, cand_34, cand_24).
- The stubs that rise above the wall line are drawn with horizontal front-face courses. Redraw them as top surface — a flat plane in the top-face value with grain running along the wall, not banded courses (cand_34, cand_35, cand_12, cand_25, cand_38).
- The timbers are painted stripes. Give each one two pixels of edge value along one long side so it stands proud of the stone, and put a driven pin or a rope lashing at both ends where it meets the wall. cand_19's pin is the only fixing in the set — do that everywhere.
- Cut the interior detail by roughly half. Magnified, cand_26 and cand_03 resolve into colour blocks divided by 1px black lines with no members in them; at 32 pixels to a floor square that becomes brown mush. Fewer, larger, nameable elements — one beam, one post, three stone courses.
- Delete cand_33 (byte-identical to cand_13). Collapse cand_03/07/10/18/30 to one tile plus genuinely distinct variants, and collapse cand_29/32. Replace the freed slots with the pieces the kit is missing: a wall end that shows its cut face, and an inside corner.
- cand_11, cand_20 and cand_37 float clear of every canvas edge with no ground line. Either anchor them to a tile edge so they join something, or cut them.
- cand_24 is two disconnected islands. Either span them with a lintel or split into two separate end pieces.
- cand_00 is in a different game — cold blue-grey, regular undamaged brick, black mortar outlines on every unit. Either repalette to the set's warm brown-grey and break the coursing with orc timber, or cut it.
- cand_09 is not a wall: isometric cube, black outline, key light from the upper left. Cut it.
