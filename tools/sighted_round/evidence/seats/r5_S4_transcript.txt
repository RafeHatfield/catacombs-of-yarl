FLIP LIST
- A's wall face sits at 72–79% of the top's value. Drop it to ~50% — the top bottoms at RGB(192,141,101), so the face field should be ~(96,70,50) — and hold that value flat down the full face height so the top/face junction is a hard break rather than a gradient step.
- A's face is 8 art px tall against a 41 art px top. Rebuild it as three 8px courses (24 art px), each with its own fill, so the wall has a standable body; the current 8px strip reads as a groove ruled into the floor.
- A's blocks are a single wash with 1px darker outlines. Delete the outline-only construction: give every block its own fill drawn from a 4-step ramp spanning ±12 values around the base, and let the joint be a value change between neighbours rather than a line drawn over an unbroken fill.
- The cream line at A y=101 is RGB(226,164,114), the brightest pixel in the frame, and runs unbroken across all 464px straight through every cell seam and behind every post. Break it at cell seams, drop it 25–30 values inside shadowed cells, and let the posts occlude it.
- A's eight posts are pixel-identical, one per cell, same x-offset, and have no top face. Vary them to 0–2 per cell at irregular x and 8–16px heights, and put 1–2px of a lighter value on each one's upper end so it reads as driven into a surface seen from above, not as a stake standing against an elevation.
