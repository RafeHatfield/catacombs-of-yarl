Give every wall cell adjacent to walkable floor a 6–8px cap band along its floor-facing edge, drawn as the top arris of the block with the course line reading over it, so the wall has a top you can see and a side that stops.
- The timber straps and pins on the wall mass float mid-tile and cross no joint. Move every strap so it spans a vertical seam between two piers, and place its pins on both sides of that seam — a strap that does not bridge anything is decoration.

Replace the ring with a bedding condition: mortar packing on two sides, a chipped lip on a third, and let the surrounding paving courses run up to and break against the slab rather than stopping clear of it.
- The corridor paving changes hue family across hard tile seams — salmon, grey-brown and olive tiles abut with no transition (visible around x≈370–500, y≈340–480 in C1). Pull all floor tiles onto one base hue and carry variation in value and wear only, so the floor reads as one surface that has been walked on rather than three tilesets pushed together.
