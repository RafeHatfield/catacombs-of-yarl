SEPARATOR: The top of the list has hardware sitting on the masonry and a floor you can pick out of the wall by value; the bottom has neither, and in the last two the wall is the same brightness as the ground.

FLIP LIST
- Give the wall an actual top surface distinct from its side: draw a 4–6px cap band along every wall edge that faces a corridor, in a colour one step lighter and one step desaturated from the wall top, and drop a 2px hard-edged occlusion shadow from that cap onto the adjoining floor tile. Do it on all four edge orientations, not just north/south.
- Stop using the same 1px salmon seam for the corridor boundary and for the decorative joints inside the solid mass. Delete every salmon seam that is not a floor/wall boundary, so that line means exactly one thing.
- Raise wall-to-floor value separation to at least 25 grey levels at full engine light, and verify it stays positive at 10% light. C3 and C5 sit at 8 and 6 and invert in shadow; that is the cull.
- Break the mirror. The map is a near-exact reflection about the player's column and about a horizontal line just above them (MAD 6.1 against a local texture std of 34.5). Generate wall detail from a per-tile hash of world coordinates with no mirrored variants in the pool.
- Kill C4's continuous ledger. Replace each full-width rail with three or four separate timbers of different lengths, each ending in a visible termination — a squared-off butt, a lapped joint over a post, or a strap wrapping the end — and offset their vertical positions by 1–3px so no two are collinear.
- Every batten and post needs a fixing where it meets the stone: a driven pin drawn as a dark hole with a bright rim on the timber and a chipped light spall in the brick beneath it, plus a 1px contact shadow on the down-side of the timber. Remove the closed black ring around each prop — it is what makes them read as stickers.
- Place the hardware where it does work. Currently the battens are scattered evenly at random angles and cross nothing. Each one must span a crack, a broken course, or a corner, so its position explains itself.
- Replace the repeating branching "twig" crack sprite. It is placed at a regular two-tile pitch in mirrored pairs and reads as bird tracks. Use stepped fractures that follow mortar courses and jog at brick ends, at irregular density.
- Remove the square-in-square medallion stamped identically at the centre of every corridor tile. Replace with a worn path: a 12–16px band of smoothed, lighter, crack-free stone down the middle of the corridor, with the untrodden margins holding grit and darker mortar.
- Delete the saturated yellow-green vegetation tufts. They are the only high-chroma pixels in the dungeon and they read as overgrowth in a place that has no light and no weather. If something must grow there, make it a desaturated grey-ochre crust in the mortar lines.
