SEPARATOR: The top of the list at least has legible masonry units with visible intervention on them, while the bottom has neither a readable block nor a hand that touched it — but none of them has a wall with a top and a side, so the whole set is a flat pattern with a path tinted through it.

FLIP LIST
- Give a wall tile a cap and a side: inset the top face 4px from the tile edge and run a darker, cooler 4px band around all four sides, so a solid cell reads as a block with a top surface rather than a patch of texture in the floor plane.
- At every wall/floor seam, bond the masonry instead of cropping it: draw a header course of full-depth stones turning the corner along the corridor edge so no brick is cut through by the seam, and drop a 2px contact shadow from the wall onto the floor slab.
- Fix every strap and pin: a 3x3 driven plate with a bright top pixel and a dark underside at each end of every iron element, plus a 1px dark line along its down-side, so the metal reads as fastened through the stone instead of painted on it.
- Break every horizontal course line at least once every three tiles with a jog, a lost stone or a mortar patch, so no joint runs unbroken across the frame; the full-width single-pixel rules in C4 are the clearest tell that nothing here was built by hand.
- Re-place the wall decoration independently on each side of the corridor: pins, bars, panels and patches are currently mirrored about the centreline (measured: mirror error 11 vs 34 baseline), so every repair has an identical twin opposite it.
- For the C3/C5 stone, draw block edges as continuous joints at least 30% darker than the stone face, on a consistent course height, and cut the wandering diagonal cracks to a third of their current density — the units currently dissolve entirely inside the light pool.
- Stop using the square framed panel in both the floor slab and the solid mass: keep it as a floor plate, and replace the wall version with a recessed niche that shows an interior side face, so the motif tells the player which cells are solid.
