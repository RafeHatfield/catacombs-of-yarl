**CULL:** Stone faces are blank at 1:1; only the joint grid and drawn-on cracks read — no material.

**FLIP LIST**
- Kill the repeating 45° hatch stamp and the vertical tally stamp; give slab faces per-slab variation — different stones, different tints, different wear states — so no two 20px windows are interchangeable.
- Add surface texture that survives the lighting: it currently multiplies to zero past ~100px from the lantern (measured std 1.24 at x 212–254 / y 302–326). Floor detail needs a light-independent floor, or higher base contrast, so the dim two-thirds of the room isn't a bare gradient.
- Make the cracks interact with the stone: step them at mortar joints, spall the lips, drop or lift the slabs on either side. Right now they cross joints unchanged (x 353, y 150) and read as a decal layer.
- Put age difference into the floor — at minimum one region of re-laid or replaced paving with mismatched stone, blown mortar and lipped edges, so the room has more than one generation on it. Repairs should read as the garrison's: driven pins, salvaged timber, hide, done badly.
- Wear the traffic line in: polish and worn-out mortar along the west→south crossing (y≈240, x 185–390, turning south at x 376–433), grit and debris banked in the dead northeast and southeast corners, so the ground states the route before the walls do.
