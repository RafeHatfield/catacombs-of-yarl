**FLIP LIST**
- Put a legible material on the tile face at 1:1 — pitting, grain, chipped arrises, aggregate — so a player can name what they are standing on without magnifying.
- Break every crack at the joints. Terminate or step it; a crack must not run unbroken across (480–580, 140–160) or (200–320, 190–215).
- Cut the stain-blob count in the lit floor from eleven to two or three, and make the survivors read as something specific rather than as generic dark splat.
- Wear the route: dish and lighten the tiles along the south corridor line (375–435, 200–470) and in the mouths of the west and north openings, and let dirt and grit collect where feet do not go, so the ground carries the traffic instead of the lamp.
- Differentiate age across the room — one patch of coarse orc repair with driven pins and salvaged timber, or one course reset out of bond — so the floor is not uniformly the same day old everywhere.
