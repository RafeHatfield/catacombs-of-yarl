The pattern worth acting on: 1, 2 and 4 are not three art passes, they are one art pass at three exposures, and the exposure is the only variable being tuned. The thing actually holding all three back is underneath that — the walls are the floor tile at lower brightness, so the level's architecture only exists where the lamp doesn't reach. Turn the gamma up on any of them and the room disappears into one continuous stone plain. Fix the wall material and the exposure question mostly answers itself; keep tuning the exposure and you will keep trading a dark figure (1) for a dissolved floor (4).
