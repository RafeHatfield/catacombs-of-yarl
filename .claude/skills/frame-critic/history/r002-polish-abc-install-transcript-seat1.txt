FLIP 4:
- The character carries no light. The sprite has a raised sword in one hand and a round shield in the other, and nothing that emits. Meanwhile the 41px-smoothed lighting peak sits at (342,499) — about two tiles to the character's left — while the sprite centre is at (408,486). Put a lantern, a wick or a burning brand in the sprite's hand and move the emitter origin onto it, so the brightest point of the frame is attached to an object.
- Redraw the wall's top surface (y 385–425 across the run). It is a soft airbrushed brown mottle with no stone divisions, no course lines and no joints, sitting 20px above floor masonry drawn as crisp rectangular slabs. Two rendering languages touching. Give the wall top the same joint vocabulary as the floor at reduced contrast.
- The timber beam at y≈425–432 is a flat 6px bar of one value running ~700px with perfectly straight edges and no ends. Where the vertical post at x≈388 crosses it there is no lashing, no overlap and no driven pin — two rectangles on the same layer. Break the beam's value across its length, and detail the crossing as an actual joint.
- Wall-base to floor separation collapses directly above the character: measured over x 380–420, the wall band (y 400–432) averages 91.0 and the floor below (y 448–468) averages 105.1 — 14 levels apart. That's the least-separated point in the whole run and it's the point the eye goes to. The only thing dividing them is a ragged 1–3px dark fringe that thins out to nothing between x≈490 and x≈550.
- The floor carries free-floating joint stubs that start and stop in the middle of a slab face and connect to nothing — a vertical bar at (622,485)–(622,500), another at (680,555)–(680,570), a horizontal one at (588,600). They're the same value and weight as real joints, so the eye can't sort joint from mark. Either terminate them at another joint or drop their weight.
- The diagonal three-stroke scratch motif appears at (570,527), (570,565) and (590,595) — the same mark, same 45° angle, same ~4px stroke spacing, three times inside a 70px vertical span. Vary the angle and stroke count, or thin them out.
- The prop at x 300–420, y 640–712 casts nothing. Floor luminance below it (41.5), left of it (65.2) and right of it (96.0) sit on a clean distance falloff curve with no occlusion term. It's the only solid object standing between the lamp and the dark, and it doesn't interrupt the light — so it reads as pasted onto the floor rather than standing on it.
