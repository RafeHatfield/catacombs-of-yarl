BEST_WHY: The floor slabs in the lit zone (roughly x 180–420, y 450–620) are actually built rather than tiled. The joints are a 1px stipple whose interruptions differ slab to slab, so no two seams read the same length; the slab interiors carry three distinct tooling treatments — short vertical chisel ticks, a fine 45° hatch, and blank stone — distributed without pattern; and slab widths vary, with double-width blocks breaking the course. Cracks that respect the grid are stamped; this one was drawn over the finished surface. The wall runs at y≈388–420 also carry thin vertical pins at x≈350 and x≈700 driven through the timber — the only thing in the deck that matches the repair vocabulary of the place.

Its left edge sits at exactly x=248 and its right edge at exactly x=343 on row 40, row 100 and row 160 — identical to the pixel, three times down the frame — and it terminates in a flat horizontal cut at y≈190.
