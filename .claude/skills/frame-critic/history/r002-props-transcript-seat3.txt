**BEST_WHY:** The masonry survives the light. In the strip at x250–520, y460–600 the horizontal joints hold a 15–19 luma dip against a base of 153, so the courses stay legible right through the hot zone instead of dissolving — the joint rows run continuously from the dark left edge to the lit centre without a break in pitch. **FLIP 1:**
- The fire pit at (598,548) emits no light. Floor two tiles west at (520,548) is luma 120 and one tile west at (555,548) is 116, but the ring at (585,532) drops to 78 and directly north of the pit at (598,505) to 86 — the floor gets *darker* as it approaches the fire. Make the pit a second emitter: warm falloff over ~3 tiles, ring stones at ~150 grading to ambient by the third tile.
- Same pit: the interior bottoms out at luma 1, pure black, while nothing else in the lit room goes below 31. Lift the pit floor to 30–45 and put ash and part-burnt timber in it so it reads as a hearth, not a hole with a decal over it.
- Same pit: the ring stones are a cool cream against a warm tan floor and read as a different asset set dropped in. Re-tint the ring to the floor's hue; let the fire brighten them, not the base colour.
- The prop at x390–425, y600–630 is a saturated red-orange checkerboard mass with no silhouette — it does not resolve into an object at any viewing distance. Redraw it as the crossed baulks used at x262–300, y600–625, in that prop's value range, or cut it.
- The same crossed-timber prop appears three times at three incompatible treatments: x262–300 (muted brown, readable), x390–425 (saturated red, unreadable), x515–560 (bright rust with a yellow highlight dash). Pick one palette and one line weight, redraw all three from it.
- The props at x390–425 and x515–560 sit outside the torch pool but are rendered at full saturation — they are brighter than the light reaching them. Multiply them by the same falloff as the floor they stand on.
- The box at x332–356, y530–565 is a pale panel with an orange scribble and no readable form. Give it a top face, two side faces, and a value break between them.
- The hatch at x310–380, y640–712 is a flat dark quad with a uniform 1px border on all four sides. Darken the north and west edges ~25 luma, lighten south and east ~15, and give it a plank top face with driven pins at the plank ends.
- No contact shadow under the figure (boots at x400–425, y505). Add a tile-wide, 3px elliptical darkening at ~40%.
