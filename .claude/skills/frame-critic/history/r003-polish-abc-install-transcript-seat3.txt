At x 250–320, y 470–560 the joints are not one treatment repeated — a course line runs solid 1px near-black for four blocks, then breaks into a broken two-value dotted line partway along the *same* course, and two blocks meet with no joint at all. Blocks carry different base values against each other rather than a single tile tinted, and the incised scratch/spall marks at ~(255,485) stop at the block edge instead of running across it, so they read as damage to *that* stone.
