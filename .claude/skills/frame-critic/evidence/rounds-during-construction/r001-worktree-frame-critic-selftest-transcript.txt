**FLIP 1:**

In the bright zone around the figure the stone joints have vanished entirely and been replaced by a 45° cross-hatch dither, while the same frame shows intact masonry courses to the right (around x 450–640, y 390–460) and in the dark. Draw one floor: run the courses under the light exactly as they run in the dark, and let brightness change only their value.
- The boundary between the hatched area and the masonry (visible clearly at x ≈ 420–500, y ≈ 380–450) is a soft diagonal that cuts through the middle of blocks — joints fade out mid-stone. Either make that boundary an architectural one (a threshold, a patch of different stone with a hard seam) or remove it.
- The hatch texture is anti-aliased and continuous-tone while the character sprite is hard pixel art with a black outline. Quantise the floor to the sprite's pixel grid so the two are the same resolution.
- The hatch runs at one fixed angle and spacing across the whole lit area regardless of tile or block. Break it up or drop it.
- The dark blocks at the right carry the same 45° four-line scratch cluster repeated on block after block at identical angle and spacing. Vary angle, length and count, or leave most blocks bare.

**FLIP 3:**

Broad pale wedges run upper-right to lower-left across the blocks above and below the figure (clearest around x 300–430, y 430–580), crossing joints as unbroken diagonals. A lamp held by a standing figure lights the floor in rings; replace the directional streak with a radial falloff centred on the sprite.
